WHO WE ARE

El Roble Academy is an educational institution located in Comayagüela, Honduras, dedicated to providing students with an environment where academic learning, personal development and values can grow together.

Our educational community promotes responsibility, respect, creativity and teamwork, encouraging students to prepare themselves for the challenges of the future.

We believe that education is not only about acquiring knowledge, but also about developing skills, confidence and character.

OUR VALUES

The principles that guide our educational community.

RESPECT

We promote respectful relationships between students, teachers and the entire educational community.

RESPONSIBILITY

We encourage students to take responsibility for their decisions, actions and academic commitments.

EXCELLENCE

We encourage students to give their best and continuously develop their abilities.

TEAMWORK

Collaboration helps students learn from one another and achieve common goals.
