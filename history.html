THE STORY OF EL ROBLE

El Roble Academy has developed as part of the educational community of Comayagüela, Honduras.

Throughout its development, the academy has focused on creating an environment where students can receive an education while developing personal and social skills.

The school community continues to value academic learning, participation, discipline, creativity and respect.
